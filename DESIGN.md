Create a breathtaking, highly detailed romantic scrapbook collage using ALL 17–20 uploaded photographs of my girlfriend as the actual source photographs.
The artwork should feel like a deeply personal handmade love scrapbook created by a boyfriend for the girl he absolutely adores — cute, romantic, playful, feminine, nostalgic, intimate, warm, dreamy and full of tiny thoughtful details.
The result should NOT look like a generic AI collage, photo grid, Valentine's Day poster, advertisement, wedding template, or overly polished graphic design.
MOST IMPORTANT — PRESERVE HER PHOTOGRAPHS
Use the uploaded photographs themselves.
Preserve my girlfriend's:
exact identity
real face
facial features
natural expressions
skin tone
hairstyle
body proportions
clothing
poses
recognizable details
Do NOT regenerate, repaint, beautify, morph, replace or redesign her face.
Do NOT create an AI version of her.
Do NOT alter her identity.
Do NOT invent additional people.
The original photographs must remain clearly recognizable and authentic.
The photographs are the emotional heart of the entire composition.
OVERALL CONCEPT
Imagine a physical scrapbook page that a boyfriend spent hours making for his girlfriend.
It should feel like:
“Here are all my favorite little moments of you, because I love looking at you and I never want to forget these moments.”
Make it imperfect in a beautiful way.
It should feel collected rather than digitally arranged.
Use overlapping photographs, slightly crooked prints, handwritten notes, paper scraps, tape, stickers, doodles and tiny observations.
PHOTO LAYOUT
Use all 17–20 photographs.
Do NOT arrange them in an equal grid.
Create an organic editorial scrapbook composition with varied scale and depth.
Use:
2–3 larger hero photographs
several medium photographs
several tiny snapshot photographs
3–5 Polaroid-style prints
one subtle film-strip section
a couple of smaller photo-booth-style images
1–2 circular or irregularly cut photo crops
overlapping photographs
slightly rotated photographs
photographs partially tucked underneath paper
photographs held down by tape
photographs extending slightly beyond their frames
Vary rotation subtly: some photographs perfectly straight, others slightly tilted.
Create realistic physical depth between layers.
Every photograph should remain visible enough to appreciate.
Never cover her face with decorations.
SCRAPBOOK BACKGROUND
Use a beautiful tactile handmade paper background.
Primary colors:
warm ivory cream soft beige blush pink dusty rose muted burgundy very subtle lavender
Add extremely subtle paper texture, fibers, imperfections, folds and natural grain.
Include torn paper pieces and imperfectly cut paper shapes.
The overall page should look like a premium physical scrapbook photographed from above.
PHOTO MATERIALS
Mix different physical print styles:
white Polaroid frames
cream borders
vintage photo prints
tiny snapshot prints
film negatives
photo-booth strips
torn-edge photographs
kraft-paper photo mats
small paper corners
translucent tracing-paper layers
Use realistic shadows between all physical layers.
WASHI TAPE & MASKING TAPE
Add realistic pieces of:
cream masking tape
translucent tape
blush-pink washi tape
tiny floral washi tape
subtle gingham/checkered washi tape
beige paper tape
Some tape should overlap the corners of photographs.
Make it look physically attached to the page.
Do not overuse tape.
HAND-DRAWN DOODLES
Add delicate imperfect hand-drawn doodles around the photographs.
Use a mixture of:
♡ tiny hearts ✦ sparkles ☆ stars flowers butterflies bows clouds smiley faces tiny suns tiny moons arrows hand-drawn circles underlines crowns cherries shooting stars little ribbons cute squiggles tiny dots “!!!” small decorative marks
Use thin imperfect black, dark-brown, muted-pink and soft-red pen strokes.
Some doodles should point toward cute details in the photographs.
Make the doodles look genuinely hand-drawn rather than digitally perfect.
Do NOT cover her face or important photographic details.
CUTE STICKERS
Add a small curated selection of adorable scrapbook stickers:
♡ pink hearts bows flowers stars butterflies cherries smileys tiny ribbons little clouds sparkles cute postage stamps tiny love-letter stickers small kiss marks mini decorative labels
Keep the sticker collection tasteful.
HANDWRITTEN LOVE NOTES
Add short handwritten captions throughout the scrapbook.
Phrases like: “my favorite person ♡”, “pretty girl”, “my sunshine”, “her smile >>>”, “my favorite face”, “how are you this cute?”, “my happy place”, “that's my girl ♡”, “ugh, I love her”, “love you, always”, “my favorite human”, “forever looks good on us”, “still can't believe she's mine”
The handwriting must feel personal, slightly imperfect and handwritten with a pen.
TINY PHOTO ANNOTATIONS
Near individual photographs: “that smile”, “look at her ♡”, “cutie”, “this moment”, “favorite”, “her eyes”, “so pretty”, “hehe”
MAIN ROMANTIC MESSAGE
“my love ♡” or “my favorite person in this whole world ♡”
COLOR PALETTE
Warm ivory, cream, soft beige, blush pink, dusty rose, muted red, soft burgundy, subtle lavender.
TYPOGRAPHY
Handwritten notes, tiny pen annotations, casual marker script, delicate cursive, vintage label typewriter text.
TEXTURE & LIGHTING
Subtle paper grain, analog film grain, realistic physical drop shadows, tactile material texture, warm ambient lighting.
